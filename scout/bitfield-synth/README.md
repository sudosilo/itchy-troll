Bitfield Synth

Bitfield Synth turns a short math formula into a live animated pixel field. It is for anyone who likes generative visuals.

You type an expression in x, y, and t and the pixel field animates from it, with an error shown for a bad expression. Presets such as tunnel, xor, plasma, and moire load ready made formulas. Audio from a loaded track or the microphone drives bass, mid, treble, and beat values. An image layer blends a picture with diff, screen, multiply, overlay, and dodge modes. Palette buttons and effects such as flash, blackout, invert, hue spin, scanlines, and glitch apply live.
