Bass chunks the pixels coarser, treble drops color depth, beats kick the mix — the photo degrades in time with the track. Tint maps it through the active theme palette.
Load track plays an audio file from your phone through the analyser (looped), or use the mic — one source at a time. Every effect is sound-modulated: Fire runs hotter on bass, Ice frosts on treble, Neon's split rides the mids; Flash strobes white on each beat, Blackout chops with the bass, Invert flips on the kick, Hue spin rotates with mids/treble. Reactivity scales all of it, and bass mid treble vol beat stay available in expressions.
