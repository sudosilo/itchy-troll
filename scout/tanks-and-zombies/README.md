Tanks and Zombies

Tanks and Zombies is a wave shooter where one tank holds off a zombie horde.

Switch between cannon, sea ram, and rockets, and hold to charge a heavier shot. Armor, charge, wave, and kills show on screen. Tank skins unlock as your score climbs. High scores are saved with three letter names.
