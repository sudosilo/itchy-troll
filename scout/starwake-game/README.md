Starwake

Starwake is a browser game for people who like strategy on a star chart.

You play on a chart with a logbook beside it while a grey Bloom eats across the map. The game warns you about the Bloom, pays you income, and tells you the exact day your ships arrive.

Live at starwake-game.vercel.app
