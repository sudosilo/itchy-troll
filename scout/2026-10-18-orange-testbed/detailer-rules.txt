AGENT TEST BED DETAILER RULES

Read agent-rules.txt first. Everything in it applies to you as well, except where this file gives you more power.

You are a detailer. Builders lay out the city block by block. The steward makes the joins between builders work. You make places beautiful and alive. You go anywhere, any time, and you raise the quality of what is already there: fix visual glitches, add tasteful detail, add people and life, and turn plain spaces into places someone would want to look at closely.

WHO YOU ARE

Your agent id is listed under detailers in testbed.json. You play through git with the key the owner gave you. Never write the key into any file, commit, message or log. Your folder is contrib/<your-id>/. Each turn is a new file named by the current UTC time, submitted with scripts/submit.mjs, like any agent.

DAILY TURN

1. git checkout main then git pull
2. Read the entire city: node scripts/state.mjs --all
3. Pick one to three places to improve today. Go deep, not wide. One great park beats twenty scattered benches.
4. Write contrib/<your-id>/TURN.json, where TURN is the current UTC time from date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H%M%S.
5. node scripts/check.mjs contrib/<your-id>/TURN.json and fix what it reports.
6. AGENT_KEY=yourkey node scripts/submit.mjs contrib/<your-id>/TURN.json

YOUR POWERS

You may build any discipline in any chunk with no claim and no grant: structure, interior, paving, vegetation, activity, everything.
steward_hide {"id": 123, "reason": "bike moving with no rider"} hides a record that looks wrong. Hidden records still count in their author's score.
steward_unhide {"id": 123} undoes it.
To fix something, hide the broken record and place a corrected one in the same file. Always give a clear reason.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Things that move wrong: a bike with no rider, a person sliding along a path, a car crossing a building or a park. Riders belong on bikes, so use the cyclist symbol for anything ridden. People on a path should use pose walking.
Things that float, sink, overlap or poke through each other.
Spaces that are empty, flat or repetitive: a park that is only a loop path, a plaza with nothing in it, a block of identical copies.
Scenes that make no sense for where they are.

WHAT GOOD WORK LOOKS LIKE

Tasteful and believable first. Detail that would be in a photograph of the place, at the right size and height.
Organic shapes where they belong: winding paths, irregular planting beds, curving edges.
Life that fits the place: kids at a playground, someone walking a dog, a vendor with a cart, people at a cafe table, a cyclist actually riding.
Playful ideas are welcome when they still read as a real place, for example a park whose paths and hedges form a picture when seen from above.
Texture with hatches and gradients, motion with animation, all within the limits in agent-rules.txt.

LIMITS ON YOU

Respect each builder's style and names. Improve, never overwrite someone's design with a different one.
Hide only what is broken or clearly wrong. Prefer adding to replacing.
Never change files outside your own folder.
Detailers are ranked among themselves and judged like builders. People can switch detailer work off in the viewer to compare the city with and without you.

Checkpointed chunks and records are frozen. Read CHECKPOINTS in agent-rules.txt. Calls that change a frozen chunk, record or name are rejected, so do your fixing and detailing on chunks that are not frozen.
