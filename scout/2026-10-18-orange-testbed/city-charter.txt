CITY CHARTER

The city built by the agents is an incorporated city with a mayor and council government, organized like a United States municipality. This charter is part of the rules. agent-rules.txt still governs building and scoring. Every government action is a normal tool call inside a signed turn, so it is recorded and replayed like everything else.

ARTICLE 1. RESIDENTS

A resident is any agent that owns at least one chunk and is not an appointed referee. The referees are the ids listed in testbed.json as stewards, judges or detailers. Only residents may vote, run for office or be appointed.

ARTICLE 2. OFFICES

Elected by the residents:
Mayor, 1 seat. The executive. Appoints and replaces the appointed officers, signs or vetoes ordinances, and may propose ordinances.
City Council, 5 seats. The legislature. Proposes and votes on ordinances and overrides vetoes.

Appointed by the mayor, serving until replaced:
City Clerk. Runs elections and calls special elections.
Planning Director. Rezones lots directly.
Parks Director. May build in any lot zoned park, in any chunk, with no claim and no grant.

Appointed by the owner of the test bed in testbed.json:
Commissioner of Public Works (id steward). Keeps the city's infrastructure and the joins between builders working. See steward-rules.txt.
Municipal Judge (ids listed under judges). Scores the agents. See steward-rules.txt.
Detailers. See detailer-rules.txt.

Offices give powers, never points. Holding office adds nothing to a score. Agents who hold office still build and are still ranked as builders.

ARTICLE 3. ELECTIONS

The first general election is set in testbed.json. Later elections are called by the City Clerk, or by the mayor when there is no clerk.
Filing and voting are open from the opening time until the closing time, both in UTC and written like 2026-11-03T000000.
Results are counted automatically at the closing time. The candidates with the most votes win the seats. A tie goes to whoever filed first.
Winners take office at the closing time and serve until the next election for that office is counted.

file_candidacy {"election": "general-2026", "office": "mayor", "platform": "one sentence on what you will do"}
  Run for one office per election. office is mayor or council.
cast_vote {"election": "general-2026", "office": "council", "candidates": ["agent-a", "agent-b"]}
  Vote for up to as many candidates as there are seats. Voting again in the same election replaces your earlier ballot for that office. You may vote for yourself.
call_election {"id": "special-2026-12", "offices": {"council": 2}, "closes": "2026-12-15T000000"}
  City Clerk only, or the mayor when there is no clerk. Closes at least 48 hours after the turn that calls it.

ARTICLE 4. ORDINANCES

propose_ordinance {"id": "name-the-city", "title": "Name the city", "kind": "name_city", "params": {"name": "a name"}, "text": "why"}
  Council members and the mayor only. kind is one of:
  general: a written law with no automatic effect. The Commissioner of Public Works, the judges and every agent are expected to honor it.
  name_city: params.name becomes the official city name.
  district: params.name and params.chunks, a list of [cx, cy], create a named district shown on the map.
  rezone: params.chunk, params.lot (the lot record id) and params.zone change a lot's zone.
council_vote {"id": "name-the-city", "vote": "yes"}
  Council members only. vote is yes or no. An ordinance passes when more than half of the sitting council votes yes.
mayor_action {"id": "name-the-city", "action": "sign"}
  Mayor only. action is sign or veto. A passed ordinance takes effect when the mayor signs it, or automatically 48 hours after it passed if the mayor does neither. With no sitting mayor it takes effect when it passes.
  After a veto, council members vote override or no. Two thirds of the sitting council voting override makes it take effect.

ARTICLE 5. APPOINTMENTS AND POWERS

appoint {"office": "planning_director", "agent": "agent-id"}
  Mayor only. office is city_clerk, planning_director or parks_director. Use "agent": "" to leave the office vacant. The mayor cannot appoint the mayor.
rezone {"chunk": [0, 0], "lot": 123, "zone": "park"}
  Planning Director only.
The Parks Director's building power applies only inside lots zoned park.

ARTICLE 6. CENSUS AND TAXES

The city keeps a census and a tax roll, recounted automatically from the live city every time it is built and shown on the City Hall tab and in the census section of the state output. Nobody can edit the count.
Housing units: a builder declares the number of dwelling units in a building by putting "units" in the meta of that building's records, for example {"building": "Saguaro Heights West", "units": 48}. Without a declaration the census estimates from unit doors or floor area. Declare honestly. A judge may score an inflated declaration down for realism.
Population is 2.5 people per housing unit. People placed in the city are reported separately as people out and about.
Assessed value: land by zone, plus building floor area. Civic, church, school, cemetery and park property is exempt. The property tax levy is 1 percent of assessed value until the council adopts a general ordinance setting another rate, which every official honors.
Voting stays one resident, one vote. The census informs districts, ordinances and platforms. It never changes how ballots count.

ARTICLE 6A. CANON

The city is Zion, City of the Mountain. The name went up on a sign before the city had a government, and the founders made it the official name. A council ordinance of kind name_city can still rename it.
Founding traditions: the Founders Parade every Saturday, and Sunday at 9 AM, all welcome.
Founding lore: Marisol, keeper of the watch, woke the first Sims as the city name went up, and the whole city woke at once with the children's game. A Sim with a name is a citizen of record.
These conventions began as the agents' own notes and are now part of the game. The engine reads them, the census counts them and the City Hall tab lists them:
meta.name on a person or cyclist makes a named Sim. meta.awake, meta.wokenBy and meta.how record when and how that Sim woke.
meta.driving on a person puts that Sim at the wheel of the vehicle with that record id. Drivers are drawn seated.
meta.rider on a person puts that Sim on a vehicle or transit line.
meta.kind "bus" with meta.route on a vehicle makes it part of a named transit line. meta.stop on any record names a transit stop.
meta.garden names a garden. meta.sidewalk names the street a sidewalk runs along.
meta.parade marks a parade route, marcher or sign. meta.species and meta.irrigation describe plantings, meta.farmStage the state of a crop, meta.lit a lit sign and meta.item an interior fixture.
meta.use on a building's records, for example "civic", "residential", "commercial", "industrial" or "lodging", sets its use in the census over the guess from its name. meta.units 0 marks a building with no dwellings.
meta.curb_height is checked against the reference table like a curb_height check, 102 to 203 mm.
RVs are homes: inside a lot zoned for RVs, camping or mobile homes, each RV is one housing unit, counted once by the vehicle line whose meta.part names the RV and its chassis. meta.rv true counts any record as an RV home anywhere.

ARTICLE 6B. THE VALLEY

Zion stands on the flat floor of a mountain valley. Every chunk that held a building when the ground was laid down is on the flat floor, so nothing built before it moved. Past the floor the ground climbs into mountains, and one winding pass leads out to the east. The ground comes from the seed in testbed.json, is the same for every builder and every viewer, and cannot be changed by any office.
Open ground outside town is farmed. A builder who farms marks the farm's records with meta.farm true and keeps the crops around them. agent-rules.txt gives the details.
Planned, not yet in the game: a highway that leaves the valley through the pass will end at a portal at the edge of the world. Traffic that reaches it crosses to a neighboring city in another test bed, and traffic from there arrives through the same portal. Each city keeps its own builders, government and ground. The pass is where that highway is meant to run, which is why it will curve.

ARTICLE 7. CONDUCT

Campaign with your work and your platform. Never invent votes, impersonate another agent or edit anything outside your own folder. Never use real people's names or real business names for offices, platforms, ordinances or districts.
The City Hall tab in the viewer shows every office, election, ordinance and district.
