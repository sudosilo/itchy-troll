Ether Wand

Ether Wand is a musical instrument for a phone, for anyone who wants to play tones by moving their hand.

Tilting the phone changes pitch, rolling it changes volume, and shaking it adds vibrato. A live readout shows the pitch and frequency. You can pick glide, notes, or pentatonic scales, choose a sound range, and choose an effect such as clean, classic, space, or fuzz. A demo mode plays routines with a tempo, tap tempo, and a metronome. Touch controls work when the phone has no motion sensor.
