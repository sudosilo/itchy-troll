6. Result

Key fob notes

Fobs and transponder keys carry no cuts to decode. They are identified by markings and radio type. Look on the back or inside the shell for an FCC ID, then look it up at fccid.io to see frequency and maker. Common types: 125 kHz proximity (EM4100, HID Prox), 13.56 MHz (MIFARE, NFC, iCLASS), car remotes at 315 MHz (North America) or 433 MHz, and car transponder chips (Texas 4C, 4D, ID46, Hitag) inside the key head.
