City Seek

City Seek is a hidden item game set in a huge city that is generated each time you play. It is for anyone who likes searching a busy scene.

You zoom into the city, look inside buildings and towers, and find the hidden items listed on a tray. There are easy, normal, and hard levels, a hint button, a time counter, and a city code you can load to replay a city. Birds fly overhead, doors swing, and tapping things gets a reaction.

Live at city-seek.vercel.app
