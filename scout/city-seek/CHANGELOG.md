City Seek changelog

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Added Music mode, chosen from the Mode switch in the menu. The city plays as a beat synced music visualizer: load a song or use the built in test beat, and the scene moves in time with the kicks, bass and hats. Views include a top down flyover where beat objects land on matching ground and cars follow streets, a train window view, a Lanes view that works like a street styled MIDI waterfall, and a Rain view with a color jar catching game. Added an action line with hit bursts for each band, an infographic layer, isometric train buildings with cutaways, and richer street detail. A hunt while it plays option, a stream hunt, and REC video recording with sound are included.

2026-11-10, v2026.11.10. Created City Seek, a huge zoomable city that is built fresh each time, with hidden items to find. Version three added a city builder with see through interiors, sandboxes, and towers, plus a tower floor view and an x-ray zoom. Ten of every hidden item are spread across the city, and tray cards count the copies you have found. Added swinging outside doors, flying birds, a Bluebird hunt, tap reactions, word bubbles, and synth sound effects with a sound toggle. Added office props, sports fields, basketball courts, traffic cones, and eighteen new hidden items.
