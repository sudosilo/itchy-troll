Hopper

Hopper is a browser game for people who like endless side scrollers.

You hop through an endless course of kinetic obstacles, with zones and combos. It keeps your best score on the device.

Live at 2026-11-25-green-hopper.vercel.app

Latest change. 2026-11-26, v2026.11.26. Flight rings, updrafts, and islands are twice as high. The ground now sits at 70 percent of the height on tall screens, and the canvas uses a lighter resolution.
