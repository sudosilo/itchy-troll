QSR Timeline

QSR Timeline is an interactive timeline of quick service restaurant history. It is for anyone who likes to see how menus changed over the decades.

The page walks through the years and shows menus along the way. Menus from before the quick service era, covering 1880 to 1920, come with photos.

Latest change. 2026-10-28, v2026.10.28. First release. Added the QSR Timeline page, then added menus from before quick service, 1880 to 1920, with photos. The photos are served from GitHub so the site upload stays small.
