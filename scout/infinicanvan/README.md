Infinicanvan

Infinicanvan is an infinite canvas toy for the browser. It is for anyone who wants to doodle and watch things grow.

The canvas zooms in and out with pinch or the toolbar, and everything stays put at any zoom. You can draw with a pen, place emoji stamps, paint, and use the cat and meow tools. Tree mode grows trees through day and night cycles, with weather and resource driven growth. Tap to water, puddles, sky, grass, and resource gauges were added in the latest version. Rulers show the scale while you zoom.

Live at infinicanvan.vercel.app
