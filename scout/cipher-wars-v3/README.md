Cipher Wars

Cipher Wars is a story game about a secret war of codes. It is for players who like history and puzzles.

You pick a side, the codemaker or the codebreaker, and play through four campaigns. Each scenario starts with a briefing and asks you to decide. Your choices earn intel points that add up to a final rank. A codex and a key figures list explain the people and methods.

Live at cipher-wars-v3.vercel.app
