Lenticular Tilt

Lenticular Tilt is a phone toy that makes a lenticular style picture from your own photos. It is for anyone who likes the tilt trick of a lenticular card.

You add a left, a center, and a right photo, then tilt the phone left and right and the picture shifts between them. You can set the tilt range in degrees and the number of lens lines, and set the center position. A manual tilt slider works when the phone has no tilt sensor.

Live at particular-lenticular.vercel.app
