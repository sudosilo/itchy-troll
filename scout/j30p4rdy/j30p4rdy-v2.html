How to play

The player with control picks a clue. Everyone races to tap their buzzer. Say your response out loud, phrased as a question.

The player holding the phone presses and holds the answer box to peek, then taps Right or Wrong. Right adds the value and takes control. Wrong takes the value away and the clue goes back to everyone else.

If the clock runs out on a buzz, it counts as wrong. If nobody buzzes, the answer shows and control stays put.

Daily Doubles belong only to the player with control. Wager up to your score, or up to the top value on the board if you're low.

D0UBL3 J30P4RDY doubles every value and hides a pair of Daily Doubles. Control starts with whoever is trailing.

F1N4L J30P4RDY: everyone above zero makes a secret wager on one category, then answers one clue.
