J30P4RDY

J30P4RDY is a lyrics quiz game in the style of Jeopardy. It is for groups who like music trivia.

Pick a genre and enter the players to start a board of songs sorted by what the lyrics mention. Players race to buzz in, with rebounds, shot clocks, Double and Final rounds, and a wager on the daily double. Clues open, answers reveal, and scores update for each player. High scores are saved to a leaderboard.

Live at j30p4rdy.vercel.app
