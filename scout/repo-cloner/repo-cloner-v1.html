LICENSE GUIDE
MIT, BSD, zlib, ISC, Unlicense, 0BSD and Apache let you reskin and sell. Keep the original copyright notice in your credits. Apache also wants a note saying you changed files.

MPL lets you sell, but any file you change from theirs stays open source.

GPL and AGPL are left out of hunts on purpose. They force your whole game open.

No license means all rights reserved. Repo Cloner never shows those.

Art, sound and fonts can carry their own license. NC or NonCommercial means you cannot sell a game that uses them. Deep Check reads those files for you.
HOW TO USE
Set your criteria on Hunt and tap Hunt. Tap Check on a card for the deep check: file tree, asset licenses, engine and a reskin grade. Tap Claude for a written verdict with what to swap and a twist idea. Tap Check All to grade every result in a row. Save the good ones to Picks, then export them or copy the names into Repo Find.
