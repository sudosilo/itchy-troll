CARCHIVE

CARCHIVE is a single player card battle game for the browser.

A title screen starts a duel against the rival. You play from a hand onto a field with equips, run a battle phase, and end your turn. Destroyed cards move to the yard. A build deck screen lets you start from a starter deck, clear, cancel, and save, and decks persist in the browser. A landing page shows duel records.

Live at carchive-nine.vercel.app
