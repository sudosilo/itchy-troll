Axiom Synth

Axiom Synth is a synthesizer that runs in the browser, for anyone who wants to make sounds on a phone or computer.

It gives you a playable synth with a choice of visual themes. The second version added new themes and fixed a round of bugs.

Latest change. 2026-10-11, v2026.10.11. Released version two of the synth with new themes and a pass of bug fixes.
