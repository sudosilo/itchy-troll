Infinite canvas

Voice draw listens to the mic and draws your voice as a line on the canvas. High notes draw high, low notes draw low, louder draws thicker, and silence leaves a gap. It records one pass across the stage and sets the sweep to the same speed, so the sweep plays your voice right back as a synth in real time. Tap Stop voice to end early.

Draw right on the stage above. Two fingers pinch to zoom and drag to pan, so you can zoom way in and draw inside your own drawing. Pan switches one finger from drawing to moving around. Backdrop puts any pattern under your drawing so you can scribble right on top of it, and Draw on it in Patterns sends the current picture here. Under Motion, Infinite zoom dives into the art forever.
