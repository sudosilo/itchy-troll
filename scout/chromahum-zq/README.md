ChromaHum Tiles

ChromaHum Tiles is a sound toy for people who like to play music with color.

The app turns colors from your camera into sound. You can use patterns and tiles as sources, and you can make your own tiles in an editor. You can zoom and pan across an infinite canvas, draw with your voice, and pick pattern backdrops.

Live at chromahum-zq.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.
