Tower Builder 2

Tower Builder 2 is a beam building tower game. It is for anyone who likes to build and test structures.

You place wood, steel, concrete, cable, and road beams between grid points within a budget. Joints are pins, so only braced triangles hold their shape. Long beams buckle when squeezed, concrete is weak when pulled, cables only pull, and heavy structures suffer in an earthquake. Levels 1 to 12 are towers tested against their own weight, wind, and an earthquake. Levels 13 to 24 are bridges where a truck has to drive across a gap. Builds and best scores are kept on the device.
