Pajamastan Invoices

Pajamastan Invoices is an invoice maker for people who trade in the micronation of Pajamastan.

It makes invoices from both the seller side and the buyer side. A letterhead tab dresses the invoice in the official Pajamastan style.

Latest change. 2026-10-12, v2026.10.12. First release. Seller and buyer invoices with a Pajamastan letterhead tab.
