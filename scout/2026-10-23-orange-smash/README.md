Smash Director

Smash Director is a mashup tool that lines up the vocals of one song with the beat of another. It is for anyone who wants to build and share mashups.

It finds the vocal only version of a song and plays it locked to the beat of another, using tempo, the beat grid, and lyric timing. Live mode listens to a song, finds matching vocals, and drops them on the bar. Autosmash chains beats and vocals on its own. Other modes cover loops, quadro smash with two beats and two vocals, a split screen, and swapping the two songs. Profiles let you save and share samples, and a band room lets phones join by code. An Android and Windows app wraps the site.
