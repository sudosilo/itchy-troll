Boba Break-Even

Boba Break-Even is a calculator and small game for a bubble tea shop. It is for owners and managers who want to know how many cups cover their costs.

The calculator shows how many cups must sell to break even, based on fixed costs per day, price per cup, and ingredient cost per cup. Plus and minus buttons change each input and the result updates live. A seven day shop game runs a week of open days, then offers a summary and another week. An info button explains the math.

Live at boba-breakeven-x8k4.vercel.app
