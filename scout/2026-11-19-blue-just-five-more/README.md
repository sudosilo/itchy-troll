Just Five More

Just Five More is a game for players who enjoy small repetitive jobs and a boss who is never satisfied. It runs in a browser on a phone or a computer.

You work through seven kinds of tasks. When a batch is done you are asked to do five more, and quitting means facing an excuse memo. The game also has fake verification, a secret lifetime ledger, and a performance review with a report card and manager comments. Each shift posts its score to the shared Score Hub, with one board per task.
