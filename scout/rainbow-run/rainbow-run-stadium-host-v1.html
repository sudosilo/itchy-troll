Floor virus

Phones find where they stand by sound. Each plays a short tick and a soft owo in its own turn, and listens for its neighbors. Seeds placed on the map start strains that spread to phones close by, and infected phones join their strain's groove.

Entry kiosks

Each kiosk is a link you open on a phone, tablet or screen at a door. It shows a code for the next guest; the guest scans it and gets a seat. Every door follows the same fill order, each code works once, and every seat records which door gave it.

Your own Supabase

A host without an account on this show's Supabase can bring their own. Make a free project at supabase.com, open the project's settings, and copy the Project URL and the anon public key. Sign in with them on the Own Supabase tab. The first sign in claims the room for that Supabase, and from then on only its accounts host that room.
