67 Idea Generator

67 Idea Generator is a pitch practice tool. It is for a person who built something and wants to explain it in sixty seconds.

You pick a style and record a take while a color wipe shows the time, with marks for problem, solution, and features. Takes are transcribed and can be read back in a voice you choose. You can sign in, group takes into projects, mark the good ones, and export them.

Live at 67-idea-generator.vercel.app
