2026-11-03, v2026.11.03. Created the app. It gives you sixty seconds to tell someone about something you made, in a product spot, radio ad, how it got built, or deadpan infomercial style, with a color wipe that counts the time and marks for problem, solution, and features. Takes are saved, can be marked good, and can be read back in a chosen voice. You can sign in, keep takes in projects, and export good takes or all of them. Your own voice service key is kept on your device in Settings.
