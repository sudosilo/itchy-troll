Torque Converter

Torque Converter is a shop tool for mechanics and anyone who tightens bolts.

Enter a value, pick newton meters, foot pounds, or inch pounds, and the page shows it in all three units as you type. A bolt spec section estimates torque from bolt size, SAE or metric, coarse or fine thread, dry or oiled, and nut material.
