Rocketbench

Rocketbench is a browser rocket simulator for people who like designing and flying rockets.

You stack parts to design a rocket, set staging, and launch it into a time step flight simulation. There is a free flight mode and missions such as First Hop, Stratosphere, Soft Return, Karman Line, Bullseye, Reentry, Round Trip, and High Ground.
