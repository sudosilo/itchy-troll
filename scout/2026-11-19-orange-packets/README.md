Packet Baron

Packet Baron is a tapping and idle game for players who like watching a silly business grow. It runs in a browser on a phone or a computer.

You squeeze out condiment packets, buy production lines, and spend money to invent new packets that should not exist. Some inventions become hit packets that pay royalties every second. After three inventions the Packet Gallery opens, where customers pay one flat price you set for any packet. Upgrades make squeezing and selling faster, and the goal is one million dollars in lifetime earnings. Progress is saved in the browser.

Checked every morning by Control Troll.
