Explode

Explode is a photo toy for the phone. It is for anyone who wants to pull a picture apart and rearrange it.

You pick a photo and tap anything in it to lift that object out as a piece. A slider spreads the pieces apart, and you can drag each one around. Undo steps back, Reset clears the photo, and the Holes button shows or hides the gaps the pieces leave behind.
