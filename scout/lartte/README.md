lARTte

lARTte is a latte art simulator for the phone. It is for anyone who wants to pour pretty coffee art without the mess.

You pour designs into a cup, and a demo mode pours each design by itself. An art generator, a gallery mode, symmetry, and cup spin help you make patterns. A paint style toolbar offers liquids, syrups, garnishes, and toppings, and a garnish shaker adds cinnamon, nutmeg, cocoa, sea salt, sugar, matcha, and sprinkles. The ARTchino tab makes blended drinks in a clear cup with add ins, layers, drizzle, whipped cream, and toppings. A coffee shop shift mode brings customers and a time attack, with tilt and shake controls and sound. Finished pours can be saved to the phone.
