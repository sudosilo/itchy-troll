How to pour
Pour lays down color where your finger is. Hold still to grow a blob, drag to push it across the cup.

Wiggle rocks the pour side to side while you drag, which builds the stacked leaves of a rosetta.

Stick is the etching tool. It lays down no color and only drags the foam, so pulling it straight through a blob turns it into a heart.

Dot drops small spots. Drag with it to lay a chain of dots, then pull the stick through them.

Pick a target, pour, then tap Finish to score it against the guide and save it to your log.
