# RTSS
Restaurant Technology Site Survey

Maintained by the scribe

RTSS

RTSS is the restaurant technology site survey, for anyone documenting the technology at a restaurant.

The survey form covers site identity, venue type, infrastructure, connectivity, point of sale, kitchen, menu boards, drive-thru, bar, table service, audio and video, payments, and notes. Counters add registers, kitchen screens, and zones. Progress updates as sections fill in, and the survey saves to the cloud and exports to CSV.

Live at rtss-sigma.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.
