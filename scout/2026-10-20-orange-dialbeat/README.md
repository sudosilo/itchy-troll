Dial Queue

Dial Queue is a drum machine that runs in the browser. It is for anyone who wants to build beats by turning dials and tapping pads.

Rows of dials set the steps, and sound families cover drums, synths, animals, kitchen sounds, and toys. Presets load whole beats in many styles. Changes made while a beat plays wait and start on the next step, beat, or bar. A Bounce button saves a loop as an audio file, and stomp box rooms let several players share one clock.

Live at 2026-10-20-orange-dialbeat.vercel.app.
