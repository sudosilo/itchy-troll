Dial Queue

Dial Queue is a drum machine that runs in the browser. You build beats by turning dials and tapping pads.

Rows of dials set the steps, and sound families cover drums, synths, animals, kitchen sounds, and toys. Presets load whole beats in many styles. Changes made while a beat plays wait and start on the next step, beat, bar, two bars, or four bars. A Bounce button saves a loop as an audio file.

How to start. Press play, then turn a dial on any row to set its steps. Pick a preset from the preset menu to load a full beat. To play with friends, open a stomp box room: everyone in the room shares one clock and one tempo, and the roster shows who has joined.
