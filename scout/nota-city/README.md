Maintained by the scribe

Nota City

Nota City is a city builder where everyone works on the same small city.

Players zone land and lay roads, power, and water on a shared 32 by 32 grid. A tick engine on the backend grows population and funds over time. The frontend has six pages, a bulldozer, and drag to paint placement. The backend runs on Express with a Postgres database.

Part of the sudosilo project set. Documentation is kept current by the scribe.
