Field Twin Glaucous

Field Twin Glaucous is a road quality logger for drivers and fleet owners. It started as Chassisquiver.

The phone logs your drive with GPS and measures vibration to score each road. Scores are relative to a baseline and adjusted for each vehicle, so no manual ratings are needed. Runs appear on a road map with share links, and each pass can be toggled. A garage holds up to 20 vehicles, with a rental flag and quick switch. It belongs to the Field Twin family and shares one sign in with the other apps in it.

Live at field-twin-glaucous.vercel.app
