Tinewood Keys

Tinewood Keys is an electric piano that runs in the browser. It is for anyone who wants to play keys on a phone or computer.

Power it on and tap keys to play notes, harder near the front of each key. Octave buttons shift the range, sustain holds notes, and the knobs turn by dragging. Computer keys also play notes.

Live at tinewood-keys.vercel.app
