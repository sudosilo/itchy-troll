Ten Turn Machine

Ten Turn Machine is a browser puzzle game for people who like building little contraptions.

You get ten turns. On each turn you draw a card and place tile parts on an isometric board. When the turns are done you run the machine and watch the ball roll through your build. The board has a paper look and a cyber look.
