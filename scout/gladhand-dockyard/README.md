Gladhand Dockyard

Gladhand Dockyard is a trailer backing simulator for anyone who likes driving big trucks and crashing things.

You back trailers into docks in a yard full of forklifts, yard trucks, and pedestrians. Hits deform both bodies, fences bend, structures cave in, and cabs can be totaled. Camera modes, a first person driver view with mirrors, a container terminal, and rail were added later. The newest version opens up a city map with signals, traffic, outages, and telephone poles whose wires come down, plus breachable buildings.
