SpaceOut

SpaceOut is a brick breaker arcade game built for phones.

Tap to start, then launch the ball and break every brick to clear the wave. Swipe, drag, or the on screen buttons move the paddle. Score, wave, and lives show at the top, and the high score is saved on the device.
