Tile-topia

The tile bag never runs out. Tap a glowing square to preview the tile there, tap it again or press Place to set it. Rotate cycles only the turns that fit that spot. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

Data lanes score 1 per tile when both ends close. Districts score 2 per tile plus 2 per chip when fully sealed. A server core scores 9 once all 8 neighbors are filled. Coolant streams score 2 per tile when both ends close. Open ground between lanes forms a Meadow. A Meadow fully enclosed scores 1 per tile plus 3 for every sealed district touching it.

Landmarks: seal a 4 tile district shaped like a heart (two single edge caps beside two corner pieces) to raise a Heart landmark worth 25. Seal a district that runs as one long chain of 4 or more corner or band pieces to lay a Strip landmark worth 2 per tile. In War it becomes an airstrip with planes. In City the towers grow taller the bigger the district and the closer to its center.

Special tiles: Spire makes its district worth 3 per tile. Relay doubles its lane. Crypto vault pays 5 on the spot. Power core gives 2 rerolls. Sealing a district of 3 or more tiles earns a reroll. Hold stashes a tile for later.

Auto builds the map by itself, choosing spots that keep future tiles placeable so gaps stay rare. It only builds inside what you are looking at. When the view is full it waits. Pan into the fog to grow more. Tap Auto again for max speed, and once more to stop.

With more than one player, a finished feature pays whoever laid the most tiles in it. Ties all get paid. Each tile carries a faint outline in the color of whoever placed it. Bots take their turns on their own.

Finishing something plays one chime per tile in it, rising in pitch, with a different voice for lanes, districts, coolant and cores. Finishing the biggest of its kind at more than 10 tiles sets off a record celebration.
