Tile-topia

Tile-topia is a tile placing game for players who like building a map piece by piece. It runs in a browser on a phone or a computer.

Each turn you draw from a tile bag that never runs out, preview a tile on a glowing square, rotate it, and place it. Finished lanes, districts, coolant streams, and cores score points, and special tiles add bonuses and rerolls. You can play alone or with other players and bots, switch skins, and use an isometric view.

Live at tile-topia.vercel.app
