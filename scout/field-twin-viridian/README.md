Field Twin Viridian

Field Twin Viridian is an app for field crews who need to capture and review what they see on site.

It reads plates and shows the reader status, including while the phone is parked. It keeps plate sightings, picks a best photo, and has a page for each plate.

Latest change. 2026-10-15, v2026.10.15. First version. Added a plate reader status and reading while parked, with an upload status. Then added plate sightings, a best photo, a pass on state and color, and a page for each plate.
