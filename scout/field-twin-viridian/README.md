Field Twin Viridian

Field Twin Viridian is an app for field crews who need to capture and review what they see on site.

It reads plates and shows the reader status, including while the phone is parked. It keeps plate sightings, picks a best photo, and has a page for each plate.

Latest change. 2026-11-23, v2026.11.23. Added a sideways layout with the camera on the left. A rotate button holds the screen sideways or upright or lets it turn. A dark screen button dims the screen and keeps it on while the camera and plate reader run.
