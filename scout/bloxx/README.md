Bloxx

Bloxx is a stage block configurator for people who plan lighting and speaker rigs. It was joined to the Stompbox band room.

You build a stage out of blocks. You can add blocks, copy them, and clear the stage. Presets give you a single tower, speakers under lights, a bridge, a stage deck, a laser wall, a gallery row, an LED wall, and a shadow wall. Light patterns include solid, rainbow, ripple, bass pulse, rain, and checker, and motion modes include bass push, facets, wave, and tilt up. Views include overhead light, lights view, see inside, top down, person for scale, and auto spin. You can join a shared room to build together.
