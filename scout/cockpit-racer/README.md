Cockpit Racer

Cockpit Racer is a driving game seen from inside the car. It is for anyone who wants a quick desert drive.

Press Drive to start a low poly 3D desert run. Drag the wheel to steer and hold gas to speed up. Traffic, deer, debris, and a police cruiser appear, and speeding near police gets you pulled over. Your best distance is saved in the browser.

Live at cockpit-racer.vercel.app
