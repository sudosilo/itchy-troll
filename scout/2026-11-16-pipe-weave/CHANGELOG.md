Pipe Weave changelog

2026-11-17, v2026.11.17. Joining a pipe now opens the wall of the pipe it joins, and the next pipe after a join waits for you. Added split, bubble, and spiral steps, merge and link steps, record and play, skins, line colors, grow rules, and a steer mode with tilt. Added lattice, diamond chain, fan, and Neighborhood presets, with more than thirty presets in all. Added a tabbed interface, an isometric view, and bridges and tunnels at crossings. Growth dots hide during play, cars and critters scale to fit, and a surprise me button picks a rule.

2026-11-16, v2026.11.16. Created Pipe Weave, a pipe drawing toy. You start a pipe with a tap, steer it left, right, or straight, and let it weave over or under other pipes. Added a grow mode with random and over under weave, then a flip end control, color modes, cars, critters, and water flow. Added roots, shadows, deep zoom, drive mode, and right angle mode. Pinching to zoom no longer draws, and undo now takes smaller steps.
