Pipe Weave changelog

2026-11-16, v2026.11.16. Created Pipe Weave, a pipe drawing toy. You start a pipe with a tap, steer it left, right, or straight, and let it weave over or under other pipes. Added a grow mode with random and over under weave, then a flip end control, color modes, cars, critters, and water flow. Added roots, shadows, deep zoom, drive mode, and right angle mode. Pinching to zoom no longer draws, and undo now takes smaller steps.
