Pipe Weave

Pipe Weave is a drawing toy for the phone. It is for anyone who likes watching pipes grow and cross each other.

You tap the paper to start a pipe, then turn it left or right, go straight, or brake. Pipes can split in two or three, end, flip their end, and weave over or under each other. Grow mode draws pipes by itself. You can choose color modes, shadows, water flow, roots, and drive mode, zoom far in or out, undo, and save the picture.
