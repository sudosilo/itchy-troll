Grow rules

How to write steps. Steps run in order from left to right, separated by commas, and every branch follows them on its own. A number after a step changes it.

Grow 100 travels that far before the next step. Split 3 divides the branch into three side by side. Kill 2 right after a split cuts two of those new branches short as stubs. Fork 40 splits into two branches that spread apart 40 degrees each way. Burst 6 shoots six branches out evenly in every direction like a star. Sprout 60 keeps the branch going and pushes out a short straight twig on each side at 60 degrees.

Turn 90 makes a sharp corner, and a negative number like turn -90 turns the other way. Arc 90 bends smoothly through that many degrees. Spiral 2 curls inward two full turns. Straighten points the branch back to the direction it started in. Wander 0 makes perfectly straight lines, wander 100 is normal, and wander 300 is jittery.

Width 70 shrinks the branch to 70 percent, and width 150 swells it. Bubble makes a round bulge. Pattern 2 plays your recorded pattern twice. Merge sends the branch into its nearest neighbor and joins it. Link keeps the branch going and sends a connector over to its neighbor. End stops the branch. Repeat starts the steps over, and repeat 3 jumps back to step 3.

Cross 4 makes a road intersection with that many arms, so cross 6 is a six way. Roundabout 4 builds a traffic circle with that many roads. Ring 80 draws a circle that far around the branch. Loom 10 weaves a square of cloth with that many threads each way.

Example: wander 0, grow 140, fork 25, grow 120, fork 25, grow 100, arc 60, end makes the Cathedral preset.
