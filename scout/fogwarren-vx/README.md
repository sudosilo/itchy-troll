FogWarren

FogWarren is a maze game for anyone who likes classic desktop puzzle games.

You walk through a maze hidden by fog and try to reach the end. Mines, pitfalls, flares, and color warps are scattered through it, and a compass helps you find your way. As you explore, a hidden picture is revealed. You can hide your own photo in the maze or use the built in pictures, and you can pick from small to colossal mazes.
