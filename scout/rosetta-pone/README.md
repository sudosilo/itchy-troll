Rosetta Pone

Rosetta Pone is a translator for anyone curious about cuneiform writing.

You can paste cuneiform signs or type a transliteration such as lugal gal. The app shows the readings and an English gloss sign by sign. A sign keyboard lets you tap signs to build your input, and each key shows its English meaning.

Live at rosetta-pone.vercel.app
