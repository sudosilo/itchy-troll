      How it grows

      The pet lives on its own clock and keeps living while the page is closed. Hunger, happiness and energy drain over time. Meals fill hunger and snacks lift mood, but snacks add weight, and an overweight pet gets sick more often.

      It poops. A messy screen raises the odds of sickness. A sick pet needs medicine, and older pets need more than one dose. Health falls while it is sick, starving or miserable, and it dies at zero.

      When something is wrong the amber light comes on and the matching button glows. Answer within fifteen pet minutes or it counts as a care mistake. At bedtime, turn the light off.

      Young pets sometimes fuss when nothing is wrong. Scold that and discipline goes up. Feed or praise it during a fit and it learns nothing. Scolding a pet that did nothing hurts its feelings. Poorly disciplined teens and adults sometimes refuse what you offer.

      It grows from egg to baby to child to teen to adult. Care mistakes, discipline and weight decide its form, and better care means a longer life. Species and colors can change at any time without touching its progress.
