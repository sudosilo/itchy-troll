Tomatomo

Tomatomo is a pocket pet game that looks like a small handheld. It is for anyone who wants a pet to look after between other things.

You hatch an egg and then care for the pet. You feed it, play with it, clean up after it, and put it to sleep. The pet grows and can evolve. You can rename it or start over with a new egg.
