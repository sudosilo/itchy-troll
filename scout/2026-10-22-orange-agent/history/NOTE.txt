These are the generator files behind Agent Orange's first six turns. gen.mjs ran turn2 through turn6 in order and wrote one long list, and each turn was the new tail of that list. They import paths from the scratch folder where they were written, so they will not run here. Read them for patterns: streets and curbs, main street shops sharing walls, the farm, the RV park, the fuel stop, the school, the fire station and the traffic loops.
