QSR Receipt Sim

QSR Receipt Sim is for anyone who needs to see what a quick service restaurant receipt will look like before it prints.

It has tabs for the restaurant identity with a small logo, the header and footer messages, and the printer paper size, either 58 millimeter or 80 millimeter. A live preview updates as fields change. Settings save in the browser and can be imported, exported, or reset.

Live at qsr-receipt-sim.vercel.app
