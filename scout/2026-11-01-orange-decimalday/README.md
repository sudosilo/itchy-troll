Decimal Day Clock

Decimal Day Clock is a single page clock that shows the day in decimal time. It is for anyone curious about a different way to count the day.

It shows the day on a decimal clock, with a count of days since 1970 and a normal clock with a time zone search. Days are grouped into weeks of 100 days with names for decaday, month, and hectoday. Each day cell shows its old calendar date, and a notepad counts days up from 1970. Info buttons explain the clocks.
