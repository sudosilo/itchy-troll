2026-11-25, v2026.11.25c. Added the shared library. Scans of an exact model now go to a shared library in Supabase that anyone can open. If the model is already built, it opens right away for everyone. If not, the scan is queued for the worker, which downloads the manuals, keeps the useful pages and diagrams, pulls out the parts, builds the model, fact checks it against the manual and publishes it as verified or draft. A quick draft shows on the phone while the worker runs, and the Shared library button in the menu lists every build and your scans in progress. The worker code is in 2026-11-22-orange-hubview-backend and runs on a schedule in GitHub Actions.

2026-11-25, v2026.11.25b. Scan a photo now pins down the exact model first. It reads badges and labels, searches the web for the spec sheet and service manual, and asks for a photo of the label when the model is not readable. The parts list carries part numbers and sizes from those sources, with links to them. Parts are drawn from a shape library of motherboards, power supplies, drives, fans, memory, graphics cards, coolers, screws, bolts, nuts, washers and standoffs, from a downloaded model when the scan finds one, or from exact spec sizes, and each part is tagged with where its shape came from. Every copy of a fastener is placed. Scans are saved by exact model, so the same model opens instantly next time, and all saved scans show under Scanned in the car picker.

2026-11-25, v2026.11.25. Added Scan a photo to the menu. Take or pick a photo of a car or anything else and Hub View sends it to the shared server, which returns a parts list with specs, threads, torque and counts. Explode it in 3D builds the object from its parts so it spreads apart like the cars, with every part tappable and searchable, and hardware store links on the buy sheet. A photo of a car Hub View already has can open that car's model at the area in the photo. The last scan is kept and shows under Scanned in the car picker.

Added an Extra explode mode that spreads out every part of every assembly. Added manual diagrams for a 2006 Crown Victoria and reshaped the Accord floor pan, trunk floor, and carpet to clear the wheels.

2026-11-22, v2026.11.22. Created Hub View, a three dimensional exploded view that started with a wheel hub and grew into a full car with 25 assemblies. Added a 1982 Accord sedan and a 2007 Prius fitted to blueprints, with a car switch, a retro spec sheet panel, and body shapes that were refined through the day. Parts show an info card in the scene, an opacity slider, and matched manual diagrams.
