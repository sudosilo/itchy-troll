Hub View

Hub View is a three dimensional car explorer for the phone. It is for anyone who wants to see how a car is put together and look up its parts.

You pick a car and spread its assemblies apart in an exploded view, or use Extra explode to spread out every part. Tap a part to get an info card with its manual diagram, part number, and shop links. You can search for parts, change the paint, adjust opacity, save a card as an image, and build a buy sheet that prints on one page.
