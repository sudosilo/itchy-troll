Fashionocalypse

Fashionocalypse is a containment game about clothes that never go away. It is for anyone who likes a strategy game with a point of view.

You play the global textile authority. Ten fashion brands spread across a globe, and you keep each one inside its home region. Killing a brand costs you approval, and too many rules make a brand go bankrupt. Laws apply to the whole world, or you can target one brand. Worn clothes move from the brand's own stores to thrift outlets to bales shipped abroad, and the dumps show up on the globe. You win early if every living brand stays contained for two straight years, or by reaching 2050 under the contamination limit.
