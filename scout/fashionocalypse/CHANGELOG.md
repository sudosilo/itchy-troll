Fashionocalypse changelog

2026-11-15, v2026.11.15. Created Fashionocalypse, a containment game about clothes that never go away. You play the global textile authority and keep ten fashion brands inside their home regions on a spinning globe. Spend Capital on world wide laws or target one brand, and tap support drops to earn more. Win by keeping every brand contained for two years or by reaching 2050 under the contamination limit.
