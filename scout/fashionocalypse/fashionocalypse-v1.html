How To Play

You are the global textile authority. Ten fashion brands are spreading across the planet like a contagion. Every garment they make is plastic that stays in the environment for generations, whether it sits in a closet, a thrift bin, a landfill, a desert, or the ocean.

Your job is containment, not extermination. Keep every brand inside its home region. Kill a brand and its workers lose their jobs and your approval falls hard. Smother a brand with too many rules and it goes bankrupt on its own.

Spend Capital on Laws that apply to the whole world, or tap a brand to target it alone. Tap the blue support drops on the globe to earn more Capital.

You win early if every living brand stays contained for two straight years. You also win by reaching 2050 with the Contamination Index under the limit and most brands still alive. You lose if Contamination reaches the limit, your approval hits zero, or more than half the brands collapse.

The Three Markets

First market: the brand's own stores and websites. This is where new product is made and sold, and it is what spreads from country to country by plane and ship.

Second market: thrift stores, off-price outlets, charity bins. Worn clothes collected in wealthy regions land here. Some are resold, the rest are baled.

Third market: bales shipped by the pallet to importing regions. Traders open them, sell what they can, and dump what they cannot. Those dumps are the desert heaps and lagoon shorelines on the globe.

Selling a product does not make it go away. Clothing in use still counts toward contamination and sheds fibers into the sea with every wash.

Burning It For Power

Some regions burn their trash to make electricity. When clothing goes in the trash, those cities are partly powered by fashion. The Fashion Power number shows how much.

Burning shrinks the landfill, but the smoke has to be scrubbed. The filters, catalysts and fly ash that catch it become their own toxic waste, shown as grey pixels next to each region's landfill, and they count extra toward contamination.
