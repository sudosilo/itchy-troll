Scorpion Supper

Scorpion Supper is an arcade game about a scorpion that eats its way to a bigger size. It is for anyone who wants a quick phone game.

Drag the field or use the arrows to steer. Blue rings are food. Amber rings need the claw. Red rings bite. When a coyote comes, hide in a coop. The screen shows your score, how much you have eaten, your level, and your feathers as hearts, and there is a pause button.

Live at scorpion-supper.vercel.app
