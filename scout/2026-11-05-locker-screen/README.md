Locker Screen

Locker Screen is a touchscreen demo of a parcel pickup locker, made for people who design or test kiosks.

Customers scan or type a six digit code, or enter a tracking number, and the locker opens. The screens come in several languages with help and a high contrast option. Technicians get troubleshooting screens for the keypad, sensors, and door.
