QSR Equivalents Engine

QSR Equivalents Engine is for restaurant operators and menu planners comparing quick service venues.

You pick a venue and see its menu items and the ingredients it stocks. Picking an item lists equivalent items that can be built from any venue's ingredients or from that venue's own stock. Gluten free tags and category colors show on the items.

Live at equivalence-engine.vercel.app
