Hoofer Sync

Hoofer Sync is a playful dance app for anyone who wants to see a band room move.

Every phone in the band room gets a rigged dancer. The tabs are Floor, Moves, Wardrobe, and Studio. You can make your own moves, pick outfits, and try famous routines, with up to fifteen dancers.

Latest change. 2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. New look: every dancer is a stick figure made of light on a blacked out stage, with light trails off the hands and feet. Added a Lights button that turns the house lights on for a bright stage. The wardrobe now picks line style and glow colors. The Studio dancer got the same look.
