Hoofer Sync

Hoofer Sync is a playful dance app for anyone who wants to see a band room move.

Every phone in the band room gets a rigged dancer. The tabs are Floor, Moves, Wardrobe, and Studio. You can make your own moves, pick outfits, and try famous routines, with up to fifteen dancers.
