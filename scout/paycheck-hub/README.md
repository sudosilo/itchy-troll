Paycheck Hub

Paycheck Hub is a budgeting helper. It is for someone who wants to see where each paycheck goes.

You pick a quick amount for a short, normal, overtime, or overtime plus travel check, or type your own. You enter the travel reimbursement, bills per check, normal check size, and floor target. Bills are paid first and the rest splits to the spokes by fixed rules. The split redraws live, and bills can be added or removed.
