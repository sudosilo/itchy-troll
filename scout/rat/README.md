Rogue Analog Transducer

Rogue Analog Transducer, or RAT, is a music player for listeners who want a visualizer for every song.

A track select hub lists songs with title and genre. Launch plays a track with its own visualizer driven by bass, mid, and treble. Pause, back to tracks, and effect controls work.

Live at rat-site-gilt.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.

Latest change. Added phone mode and device detection. Tracks now come from a shared library, and a Haunt Me tile was added to the hub.
