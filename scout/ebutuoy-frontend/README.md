ebutuoy

ebutuoy is a video discovery app that favors videos almost nobody has seen. It is for people who want to find small creators.

You pick topic, local, or roulette mode. It plays thirty second samples of videos with very few views from small channels, and it never repeats a video you have already seen.

There is also a TV mode with a channel dial from 1 to 1,000,000, where each number is its own channel.

Live at ebutuoy.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.

Latest change. 2026-11-09, v2026.11.09. Added a TV mode to the launch screen. A cream colored TV cabinet has a knob that speeds up when you spin it fast, channel up and down buttons, and a box to type a channel number and press go. Static plays while it tunes and the channel number shows on screen. Each number plays that channel's videos newest first in thirty second samples, then loops. The last channel is remembered on the device.
