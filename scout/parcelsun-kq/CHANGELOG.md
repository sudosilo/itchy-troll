Parcelsun changelog

2026-11-12, v2026.11.12. Doors are now fixed slot sizes measured off a real bank, on a column of 29 units, and each column in the Layout card is a list of slots you edit. Slot heights were fitted from close photos of a version 3 bank. The starter module is now two columns, a screen column beside a full column of doors, and oversize modules from Field Twin land on the right end. Save to Field Twin keeps the whole bank slot by slot. Every card under the 3D view is now a drop-down, closed to start, and the page remembers which are open.

2026-11-11, v2026.11.11. Added shade blockers. A wall, building or tree is a span of bearings with a top angle, or a height and distance, and the locker gets no direct sun while the sun sits behind it. A sky chart shows the blockers against the day sun path, and Blocker shade counts the hours. Added the Field Twin card. Find the locker I am at loads the closest saved locker with its facing, generation, doors, placement and blockers, blanks filled from the latest visit survey. Save to Field Twin writes adjustments back.

2026-11-10, v2026.11.10. Added a Locker Screen kiosk to Parcelsun. Codes and scans swing a door open on the 3D model, doors can be tapped to open and close, and the tech repair flow opens the matching door. Versions two to five are now set up, with a screen on two and three and a Bluetooth phone pickup on four and five. Added a wrap graphic that stretches a photo across the bank front, or joins several photos side by side.

2026-11-09, v2026.11.09. Parcelsun version five adds a layout editor in the 3D model, door numbers shown on the doors, and a door numbers card with per column numbering, a skipped starter, and door search. Added version four and version five styles, Q units, and outside and inside corners.

2026-10-13, v2026.10.13. First release, built over four versions in one day. A parcel locker solar heat model, then a board strip, hat shadow, console gear heat, and back view. Then a field style locker look with a four module default, and finally even service seams, cabinet louvers, and console panel detail.
