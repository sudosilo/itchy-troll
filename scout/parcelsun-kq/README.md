Parcelsun

Parcelsun is a heat model for a parcel locker sitting in the sun, for anyone studying how hot a locker gets.

It models solar heat on the locker with a four module default and a field style locker look. It shows a board strip, hat shadow, console gear heat, and a back view. Version four added even service seams, IT cabinet louvers behind the screen, and console panel detail.

Latest change. 2026-11-12, v2026.11.12. Doors are now fixed slot sizes measured off a real bank, on a column of 29 units, and each column in the Layout card is a list of slots you edit. Slot heights were fitted from close photos of a version 3 bank. The starter module is now two columns, a screen column beside a full column of doors, and oversize modules from Field Twin land on the right end. Save to Field Twin keeps the whole bank slot by slot. Every card under the 3D view is now a drop-down, closed to start, and the page remembers which are open.
