Parcelsun

Parcelsun is a heat model for a parcel locker sitting in the sun, for anyone studying how hot a locker gets.

It models solar heat on the locker with a four module default and a field style locker look. It shows a board strip, hat shadow, console gear heat, and a back view. Version four added even service seams, IT cabinet louvers behind the screen, and console panel detail.

Latest change. 2026-11-11, v2026.11.11. Added shade blockers. A wall, building or tree is a span of bearings with a top angle, or a height and distance, and the locker gets no direct sun while the sun sits behind it. A sky chart shows the blockers against the day's sun path, and Blocker shade counts the hours. A locker opened from its Field Twin locker page arrives with its name, spot, facing, generation, door count, placement and blockers filled in.
