Manual Rebuild changelog

2026-11-25, v2026.11.25. Created Manual Rebuild, a tool that turns manual figures into parts. Version 2 added a parts studio that traces figures into polygon parts and exports them as SVG. It also shows an exploded 3D view with a spin control and an explode slider.

2026-11-25, v4. Drag a figure up off a book page and it rises into a 3D wireframe you can spin. The drawing's line work is traced into vector lines and each part gets a front face, back face and side edges. Sizes printed on the drawing are read and pinned to the edge they measure, with a leader line, and any size can be moved to another edge or removed. Front, Side, Top and Angle views, a Depth slider, and a faint copy of the page behind the model. The parts studio has a Lift button for the same view.
