Welcome to the Madness

Welcome to the Madness is a short cooking game for the browser and phone. It is for anyone who wants a quick rush of a game.

Orders arrive on tickets. Tap a station to start an item, then tap again when it flashes green to pull it before it burns. Serve sends a full ticket before the customer walks out. Score, served, and three hearts show, and three walkouts end the shift. Later levels add a doneness ring, perfect pulls, combos, rush levels, sound, and pause.

Live at welcome-to-the-madness.vercel.app.
