Field Study

Field Study is a study app for people learning field training material.

A sets screen lists training modules that unlock in order. Flashcards show each card with flip and shuffle. Quiz mode checks answers and shows the result, with a retake. Matching and multi pick drills check answers and run again. Progress saves in the browser and can be cleared.

Live at field-study-pi.vercel.app
