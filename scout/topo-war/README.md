Territory Control

Territory Control is the first version of what became Topo Wars. It is a turn based war game for people who like strategy on a map.

It has soldier and artillery pieces, undo, a center button, and end turn. You choose a battlefield size or use your location, and the game reads the terrain before the battle starts. A menu holds a how to play guide and a battle log. Later versions live in the topo-wars repo.
