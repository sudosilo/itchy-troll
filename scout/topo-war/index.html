How to play

You command the blue side. The enemy commands red. Each turn you gain 1 supply, your commander can step one cell in any direction, every soldier can step once, and every ready artillery piece can fire once. Do these in any order, then press End turn.

Every cell you step on becomes your territory. You only score by attacking: capturing an enemy cell is worth its tier (1 in valleys up to 5 on peaks), destroying a soldier is 5, destroying artillery is 8, taking the enemy HQ is 20 once, and sealing off enemy cells inside a closed loop of your territory is 10 and flips everything inside.

Soldiers cost 1 supply and artillery costs 2. Both are placed on your own territory and act from the next turn. Artillery never moves. Its range is 2 plus the tier of the cell it sits on, so a peak gives range 7 and a valley gives range 3. Artillery can hit enemy cells and enemy units but cannot hurt a commander.

Moving your commander or a soldier onto the enemy commander wins instantly, and the enemy can do the same to you. Cells shown in orange during a move are within reach of an enemy piece next turn. First to 150 points wins, otherwise the higher score after 40 rounds.

Tap any cell to read its terrain. Pinch to zoom. Undo takes back moves made this turn.
