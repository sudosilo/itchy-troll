Suno Forge

Suno Forge is a song builder for people writing songs in AI music generators.

Pick a genre, a mood, and a song structure, and it drafts lyrics and a matching style string with Claude. EDM options cover drop types and sidechain pump strength. Copy buttons copy the lyrics, the style, or everything at once.

Live at suno-forge-kappa.vercel.app

Checked every morning by Control Troll, an automated test system that loads every app and clicks through its features.
