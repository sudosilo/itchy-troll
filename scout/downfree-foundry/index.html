Library

Full video parts and stop motion sets are marked. In Composite, pick a full video as Base so it supplies the road, and stop motion sets layer on top. Save to phone copies an item out to Downloads. The app keeps its own copy either way.

1. Clips

Add parts from the Library above, or pick video files from the phone. The clip marked Base supplies the road.

2. Line up

Each drive started recording at a different spot on the road. Line up makes every clip start at the same spot. Tap Auto line up first.

The picture flips between the Base clip and one other clip. When they are lined up, the road, signs and hills stay still and only the cars change. If things jump, nudge that clip's Start slider.
