Tapperator

Tapperator is a drum kit played on a steering wheel, for anyone who wants to tap out beats.

You tap a steering wheel as a drum kit after a five round calibration. It has a four tab looper and syncs with the stomp box room. Each pad has five sample sets: soft, hard, medium, mixed, and rhythm. You start with two pads and add more when ready.
