BPM Finder

BPM Finder listens to music through the phone microphone and tells you its tempo. It is for DJs, musicians, and anyone who needs to know how fast a song is.

A big dial shows the tempo in beats per minute, with the exact value under it. Meters show the microphone level and how confident the reading is, and the dial flashes on each beat. A tap button lets you tap the tempo yourself. Press listen and hold the phone near the speaker.

Live at bpm-finder.vercel.app
