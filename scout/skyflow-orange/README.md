Sky Flow

Sky Flow is a short crane game for the phone. It is for anyone who wants a quick round of stacking.

You run a tower crane and stack crates as high as they will stand before the clock runs out. Rounds are timed, the game keeps score and plays sound, and a leaderboard shows the best results.

Live at skyflow-orange.vercel.app
