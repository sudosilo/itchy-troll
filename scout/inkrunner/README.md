Inkrunner

Inkrunner is a driving game for anyone who likes arcade tricks in a drawn world.

It is a driving sim in a comic ink style. You can pull air tricks like a kickflip, a 180, and a 360. There is a track loop with barricades, a crash and reset, a lap ghost, outer walls, a wider road, zoom out, and a brace button. You can also drive freely outside the walls.
