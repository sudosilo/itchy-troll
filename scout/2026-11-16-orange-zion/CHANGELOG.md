Zion Wakes changelog

2026-11-23, v2026.11.23. Fixed four playtest bugs. The first load now retries the city fetch and shows a Try again button instead of hanging. Moving a place now ends after the tap and says where it moved, and a blocked spot cancels with the reason. Name it opens a field inside the game instead of a browser pop up. Street labels are now drawn once per shared edge.

2026-11-16, v2026.11.16. Created Zion Wakes, a story and map toy that loads the Agent Test Bed city. You pan and zoom the map and read a short story beat. You can pick a place to move it, name it, approve it, or reject it, and start over with New start. Two follow up builds were triggered to get the first deploy live.
