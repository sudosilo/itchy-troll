Zion Wakes

Zion Wakes is a story and map toy built on the Agent Test Bed city. It is for anyone who wants to wander a city that keeps resetting.

The app loads the city from the Agent Test Bed and shows it as a map you can pan and zoom. A story panel tells what is happening, and when you pick a place you can move it, name it, approve it, or reject it. A New start button begins again.

Live at 2026-11-16-orange-zion.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll, an automated test system that loads every app and clicks through its features.
