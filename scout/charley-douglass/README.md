Laugh Box

Laugh Box is a laugh track instrument for the phone. It is for anyone who wants to play an audience like a keyboard.

Each key plays one laugh, and holding it keeps the audience going until you let go. Every built in sound is made live on the phone from nothing but code, with no recordings. You can load your own recording onto a key. A one bar pattern lets you drop keys on steps and play them on a room loop. A stomp button and level control shape the crowd, and rooms let other phones join in.
