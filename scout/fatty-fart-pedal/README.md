Fatty Fart Pedal

Fatty Fart Pedal is a joke instrument for anyone who wants to make a room laugh.

You stomp a pedal to play a sound cart. You can pick the size, add echo, and set the level. A step pattern plays one bar on a room loop, and stomps snap to the loop. You can play alone or join a room with a code to play together.

Live at fatty-fart-pedal.vercel.app
