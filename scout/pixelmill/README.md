PIXELMILL

PIXELMILL is a toolpath simulator that cuts pixel art with machine style programs. It is for people who like watching a mill work.

It runs endless programs such as raster, spiral, hatch, concentric, pocket, lissajous, star, and cityscape. You can pause, skip to the next program, nudge the target, toggle fill, flood, depth, and fade, change pen size, and switch city views.

Live at pixelmill.vercel.app
