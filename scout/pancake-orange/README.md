Pancake Orange

Pancake Orange is a quick arcade game for a phone.

Pancakes cook in slots and change color as they burn. Tapping one in time saves it, and a burnt one adds a strike. The saved count, strikes out of five, and board level show on screen. The board grows with the level. When the game ends you can play again.
