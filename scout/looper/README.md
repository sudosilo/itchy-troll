Looper

Looper is a loop recorder for anyone who wants to tap out beats and layer sounds on a phone or computer.

You tap sound pads to play synth and drum sounds, record a loop, overdub, undo the last dub, and export a loop as a sound file. It can join a shared band, and the third version added band grooves.

Live at looper-sooty.vercel.app
