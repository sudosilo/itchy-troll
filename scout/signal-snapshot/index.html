[signal snapshot]

This panel watches the price bars the ticker tracker already loads. Open a ticker in the tracker and its bars are sent here. Close the tracker and nothing is sent.

Each time a new bar completes, the backend checks it against 61 candlestick patterns from TA-Lib, RSI leaving oversold or overbought, a 5 bar momentum breakout, and MACD crossing its signal line. When one of those fires, it saves a snapshot: the rule, the numbers that triggered it, the chart up to that exact bar, and the tracker's supporting values at that moment, like the virality score and recalls. Snapshots are append only and never edited.

Later, once enough bars have come in after a signal, it gets graded. A bullish signal wins if price rises by the target before it falls by the stop, loses if the stop comes first, and times out if neither happens in time. Bearish signals work the same way in reverse. A small trading cost comes off every result. The report tab adds this up per rule, so over time it becomes clear which rules earn their place.

This is observation only. It never places an order and it is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
