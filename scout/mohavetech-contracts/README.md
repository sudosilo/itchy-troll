Contracts

Contracts is a simple app for making a signed agreement between two people. It is for small deals that need proof of what was signed.

You draft a contract, with wording help that can clean up your text or draft from a plain description. A draft link copies so the other party can open it. Both parties sign by drawing on signature pads. The fingerprint of the signed contract is recorded on Polygon, with a retry if recording fails. Verified records open by link and give a signature card image to download.
