How a contract gets made

four steps, one session
01

Write it

step 01 Pick a contract type, fill in the terms, let the assistant clean up the wording. Both parties read the same screen.
02

Sign it

step 02 Each party applies an e-signature in the browser. The signed document is locked the moment both signatures land.
03

Notarize it

step 03 The signed document is hashed and the hash is written to the chain. A transaction record comes back as proof.
04

Print it

step 04 A card is generated with a QR code to the record and a PIN. Both parties sign the back by hand.
