Polygon Starfighter changelog

2026-11-26, v2026.11.26. Added a Story mode campaign with briefings and ship unlocks. Online scores arrived with a leaderboard and a score server, and the game now connects to it. Story mode now follows the Starwake storyline.

2026-11-25, v2026.11.25. Created Polygon Starfighter in the MobbyHill look with tilt controls. Later versions added a wireframe landing screen, five ships, lasers with barrel heat, enemy ships, Mothership Hunt, dogfight AI, a lead marker, turrets, three maps, Territory and Long haul modes, and homing mines. A Hover race mode brought terrain, a track, gates, and lap times. Also added were a cockpit view toggle and ore harvesting with outpost turrets in Territory.
