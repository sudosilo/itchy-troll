GroundCore

GroundCore is a planting planner for gardeners and small growers in the Mohave Valley desert region.

A row spacing calculator works out plant and row counts for a plot, and picking a plant fills in its recommended spacing. A plant lookup shows a profile for each plant, and a browser filters plants by category. A sandbox map lets you place, move, and delete rows, structures, and drives, then put the layout on a real map using GPS.

Live at groundcore-nine.vercel.app
