Circuit Visualizer

Circuit Visualizer is a music visualizer for anyone who likes watching sound. It runs in a browser.

Audio from a loaded track, the microphone, or a demo drives the visuals. Circuit mode builds a random circuit board that lights its traces on beats. Other modes include ASCII bars, matrix rain, and a terminal with several themes, and the Bitfield mode was added from another app. Fullscreen and the menu work.

Live at circuit-musicfx.vercel.app
