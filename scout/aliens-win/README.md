The Aliens Win

The Aliens Win is a shooter where you fly a UFO. It is for anyone who wants a quick arcade round.

Pick a ship, then press Initiate Harvest. You fly the UFO and fire lasers at targets while enemy shots return. Waves advance with a banner, and a game over screen lets you try again.

Live at aliens-win.vercel.app
