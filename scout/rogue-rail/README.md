Rogue Rail

Rogue Rail is a fast arcade shooter for anyone who likes playing to a drum and bass beat.

The game loads with its music built in and starts a run down a corridor on rails. It has pause, mute, hull repair, a countdown, and a saved best score.

Live at rogue-rail.vercel.app
