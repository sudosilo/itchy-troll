KVS Roulette

KVS Roulette is a video roulette for people who want to land on a random moment from a show library.

It scans the episode library and offers shuffle clips, random episodes, and seasons modes. Clip length can be 15 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 2 minutes. Controls cover play, pause, next, back, forward, and start over, and a seek bar scrubs full episodes.

Live at kvs-sigma.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.

Latest change. Version 2 plays full episodes and adds a random mode, seasons, and backup sources. Added a seek bar, start over, back and forward jumps, and fullscreen that keeps the controls.
