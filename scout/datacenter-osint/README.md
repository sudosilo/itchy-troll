Data Center OSINT

Data Center OSINT is for researchers who want to see what surrounds a data center site using open sources.

A map shows data centers, thermal power plants, heavy industry, cooling towers, and drought layers that toggle on and off. A region picker covers California, Arizona, Nevada, the Southwest, or the whole country. Live satellite imagery drapes over the map in true color, water vapor, and infrared, with a loop of the last day. Moving wind trails show on the map.

Live at datacenter-osint.vercel.app
