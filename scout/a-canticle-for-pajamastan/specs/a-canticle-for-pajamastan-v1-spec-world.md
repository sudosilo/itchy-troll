A Canticle for Pajamastan, world objects spec, version one.

This document describes the interactive objects on a floor and the switch flags that tie them together. It is restated in our own terms from observed behavior of a classic console action RPG. Every radius, timer and damage lives in the world tuning file, and the values there are ours.

Each floor has a table of numbered switch flags, all off when the floor starts. Objects come in two roles. Triggers turn flags on or off, and reactors watch flags and change when they change. A trigger never talks to a reactor directly. They only share a flag number, so any trigger can drive any reactor, and a quest script can set or read the same flags.

The trigger kinds are these. A step switch turns its flag on while a player stands on it, and can be set to stay on once pressed. An attack switch turns its flag on when hit. An item switch turns its flag on when a player uses a named item while standing next to it, and takes the item. A timer switch watches another flag, and when that flag turns on, it starts a countdown that ticks audibly once per second. When the countdown runs out, it turns its own watched flag back off, so players must race through what it opened. A sensor turns its flag on when any player enters its area. A chat sensor turns its flag on when a player standing in its area sends a chat message containing its keyword. Room clears and quest scripts can also set flags.

The reactor kinds are these. A door opens when all of its listed flags are on, and closes again if any turns off, unless it is marked to stay open. A light door has several lamps, each tied to its own flag, and each lamp lights as its flag turns on, so players can see how close they are. It opens when every lamp is lit. A laser fence blocks a line and hurts on touch while its flag is off, and drops when the flag turns on. A fog zone is a round area that is either a sight blocker or a poison cloud that ticks damage on players inside. It clears when its flag turns on. A bridge is a walkable strip that only exists while its flag is on. A warp only works while its flag is on.

Some objects stand alone. An explosive barrel bursts when hit or when its flag turns on. The burst damages everything in its radius, players and enemies alike, and sets off any other barrel inside that radius after a short delay, so lines of barrels go off in a chain. A breakable rock blocks the way until it takes enough damage. A healing point restores health and spell points to a player standing on it, and can be single use. A box was covered in the loot spec.
