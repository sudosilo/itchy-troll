A Canticle for Pajamastan, comeback move spec, version one.

This document describes the super meter and the comeback moves it powers. It is restated in our own terms from observed behavior of a classic console action RPG. Meter rates, chain bonuses and move powers live in the comeback tuning file, and the values there are ours.

Every player has a super meter that fills toward full. It charges from damage taken, at a high rate so getting beaten on builds a comeback, and from damage dealt, at a lower rate. A downed player's meter is frozen until they're revived.

Each player equips one comeback move out of a set they've unlocked. The moves are a big area strike, a long line strike, a full party heal, a party guard that makes allies invulnerable for a short time, and a meter gift that fills a chosen ally's meter.

A player with a full meter can start a chain. Starting a chain opens a short join window. Any other player in the room with at least a join threshold of meter can add their own equipped move to the chain during the window, spending their meter. A player can also donate meter to someone else's chain without adding a move, which raises the chain's power. When the window closes, the moves fire in the order players joined, one after another.

Chains scale up with more players. Each move in a chain is multiplied by a chain bonus that grows with the number of moves already fired in it. The final move of a full chain of four is the strongest thing in the game. Damage dealt by comeback moves never charges anyone's meter.

Each comeback move gets its own power from the level of the player who fired it, using a base amount and a per level amount. Strike moves hit every enemy in their shape once, ignore evasion and accuracy, and are reduced by defense at half the usual rate.
