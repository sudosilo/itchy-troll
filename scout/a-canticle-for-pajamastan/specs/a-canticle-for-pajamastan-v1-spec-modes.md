A Canticle for Pajamastan, modes spec, version one.

This document describes difficulty tiers and the special play modes. It is restated in our own terms from observed behavior of a classic console action RPG. Every multiplier and rule lives in the modes tuning file, and the values there are ours.

There are four difficulty tiers. Each tier multiplies enemy health, power, defense, accuracy and evasion. It adds a flat number of levels to enemy level for experience purposes, and it raises the area depth the loot system reads. Each tier also has its own trap damage scale, and the higher tiers unlock their own rare tables. A room can only be made on a tier when the room creator's level is at or above that tier's minimum level.

Challenge mode is a set of stages played in order by a fixed party. Every player is set to the stage's level and handed the stage's fixed loadout of weapon, armor, consumables and spells, so their own characters don't matter. Enemies drop nothing. The party shares a pool of lives, and when a player dies with lives left, they respawn at the last checkpoint. When the pool runs out, the stage is lost and can be retried from the start. Each stage is timed, and clearing it under a target time earns a rank. A stage clear rewards a prize based on the best rank across all stages.

Battle mode is player versus player on a set of rules picked before the match. The rule set chooses free for all or teams, a time limit or a kill target or both, a lives count, whether players keep their own gear and level or use a fixed level and loadout, and the trap supply. Score is the sum of kills, damage dealt, damage taken and deaths, each times its weight. A player killed with lives remaining respawns at a random spawn point after a short delay, invulnerable for a moment.
