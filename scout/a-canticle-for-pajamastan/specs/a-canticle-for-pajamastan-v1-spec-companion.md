A Canticle for Pajamastan, companion spec, version one.

This document describes the floating companion each player can raise. It is restated in our own terms from observed behavior of a classic console action RPG. Feed tables, thresholds, forms and trigger odds live in the companion tuning file, and the values there are ours. The companion's names and forms are ours.

A companion is equipped like gear and floats beside the player. It has four growth tracks, called grit, guard, aim and wits, which add to the player's power, defense, accuracy and mind. Each track has a level and progress toward the next level. The companion's total level is the sum of its track levels, up to a total cap.

Companions grow by being fed consumables. Each consumable has a feed entry for each companion form that adds or subtracts progress on each track, and also changes bond and smarts. Feeding has a cooldown, so a player can't dump a whole bag at once. Progress past a full level spills into the next level, and negative progress can pull a level back down, but never below zero.

Bond and smarts both run from zero to a cap. Bond rises through feeding and falls a little when the player dies. Smarts rises through feeding foods it likes. Bond sets how strong the companion's helpful actions are, and smarts sets how likely they are to happen.

A companion changes form as it grows. At set total levels it evolves, and the new form is picked by which tracks are highest, with ties broken by the player's archetype. Later forms also depend on whether specific track levels are odd or even, so careful feeding can steer the outcome.

Companions act on triggers. The triggers are entering a boss room, the player's health dropping below a threshold, the player dying, and the player's super meter filling. Each form has an action for each trigger, like casting a heal, granting a guard up, reviving the player once, or making the player briefly invulnerable. The chance that a triggered action happens comes from smarts tiers, and its strength comes from bond. A companion also builds its own super meter over time and grants a comeback move from a set it unlocks at evolution milestones.
