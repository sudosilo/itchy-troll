A Canticle for Pajamastan, town spec, version one.

This document describes the people who live in town and how they behave. It is restated in our own terms from observed behavior of a classic console action RPG. Speeds, ranges and timers live in the town tuning file, and the values there are ours.

Every town character has a role. Walkers wander between waypoints along paths, pausing at each one for a random time. Idlers stand in one spot and play small idle motions. Guards walk a fixed patrol loop and stop to face anyone who comes close. Service characters, like the shopkeeper, the appraiser, the nurse and the quest clerk, stand at their counters and open their service when a player talks to them inside their service range.

Every character turns to face a player who comes within their notice range, holds that look while the player stays near, and goes back to what they were doing after the player leaves. A player can talk to any character. Talking plays one of the character's lines, cycling through them so repeat visits hear something new. Some lines change once story flags are set, so the town reacts to progress.

Walkers keep a little space from each other and from players, steering around anyone in their way instead of walking through them. Children walk faster and pause more often. Small animals scatter when a player runs through them and settle back after a while.
