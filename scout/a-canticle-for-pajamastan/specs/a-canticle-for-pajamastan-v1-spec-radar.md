A Canticle for Pajamastan, radar spec, version one.

This document describes the small map in the corner of the screen. It is restated in our own terms from observed behavior of a classic console action RPG. Ranges and colors live in the radar tuning file, and the values there are ours.

The radar is a top down map centered on the player and turned so their facing points up. It shows the shapes of the rooms and halls the player has seen, and leaves unseen parts of the floor blank. A room counts as seen once the player enters it or stands in a hall leading into it.

Icons sit on the map. Teammates, enemies, items on the ground, doors, switches, warps and quest markers each have their own icon and color. Enemies only show inside the radar's live range around the player, and nothing beyond that range moves on the map. Locked doors show as closed until their flags turn on.

Some objects are hidden from the radar until a detector is used. Hidden traps and mines are the main ones. A detector tool reveals every hidden icon within its range for a while.

A full map view opens with a button and shows the whole seen floor, north up, with the same icons, at a larger size.
