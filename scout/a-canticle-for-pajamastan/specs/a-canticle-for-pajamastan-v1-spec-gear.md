A Canticle for Pajamastan, gear spec, version one.

This document describes equipment slots, requirements and how gear adds up into the stats combat uses. It is restated in our own terms from observed behavior of a classic console action RPG. Requirements, slot counts and charm effects live in the gear tuning file, and the values there are ours.

A character has a weapon slot, a body armor slot, a shield slot and a companion slot. Body armor has a number of charm sockets, from none up to four, rolled when the armor is made. Charms go into those sockets and are lost from the sockets when the armor is taken off.

Every weapon family can be used by some classes and not others. Each weapon also has a stat requirement, usually power for melee, accuracy for guns, and mind for staves. It's set by the weapon's tier, and a character below the requirement can't equip it. Armor and shields have a level requirement set by their tier. Some gear is locked to one archetype or one class.

Final stats are built in layers. The class stats for the current level come first, then stat boosting consumables, then the companion's track bonuses, then the flat bonuses on armor and shields, then charm bonuses, and last the percentage buffs and debuffs from the status spec. Weapon power and spread come from the weapon's tier and refinement. Its bonus slots and special ride along into combat.

Charms come in kinds by star count. Low star charms add a flat amount to one stat, middle charms add to two stats, and high star charms grant an effect instead, like slowly regenerating health or spell points, resisting one status, or adding a small amount of every stat.

Unequipping is free. Equipping is blocked during a fight action and while a hard lock status is active.
