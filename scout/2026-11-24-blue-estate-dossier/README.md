Estate Dossier

Estate Dossier is a web app for people who want to see where their personal information is exposed and work through cleaning it up.

It shows exposures on a radial map and in a list. You can triage each one, keep a task list, run an intake wizard, follow each broker through its steps, and export the results. You sign in to an account, and your data is stored encrypted for that account and synced.
