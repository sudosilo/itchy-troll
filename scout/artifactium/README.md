Artifactium

Artifactium is an archive of found objects. It is for people who collect and catalog things they come across.

Objects are shown in a flowchart tree that you can explore. Records are kept by a backend, and contributors sign in with an email code and choose a handle.

Live at artifactium.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll, an automated test system that loads every app and clicks through its features.
