Campus

Campus is a phone friendly learning tool for people who want to learn from videos and keep a record of what they build.

You save a name label with no password. Start stumbling plays a random video with skip and done buttons, and you log what you did with it. Building counts more than watching, and your transcript keeps the score in buckets with credits. Course mode loads a course list and plays its videos in order. You can add channels and playlists as sources and pick education genres such as history, disasters, and safety. Open in YouTube opens the current video.

Live at campus-ebutuoy.vercel.app
