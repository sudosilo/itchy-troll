Ascend

Ascend is a vertical platformer for phones and browsers. It is for anyone who wants a quick climbing game.

The player climbs endless generated platforms, and some of them move. Touch zones on screen steer and jump. Coins, gems, gliders, rockets, and powerups appear during the climb. Wind streaks, clouds, and milestone banners show up as height increases. Progress saves in the browser.

Live at ascend-pearl-ten.vercel.app
