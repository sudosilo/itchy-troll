Lob Hill

Lob Hill is a browser artillery game for people who like aiming shells from a tank turret.

You sit in a dug in turret and aim with a wheel, dials, and pedals, with an orthographic map beside the seat. You can duel the computer tank or a friend by pass and play. You can also hold the hill against jeeps, Bradleys, and androids.
