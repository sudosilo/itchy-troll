CoffeeKit One

CoffeeKit One is a coffee shop training game for people learning how a drive-thru and lobby line works.

Orders show on a kitchen display with drive-thru and lobby queues. You click the highlighted station to advance each step, including milk selection, and the next ticket loads when an order completes. Cleaning rounds have stations to clean and a sink to finish, and the game counts orders and score.

Live at coffeekit1.vercel.app
