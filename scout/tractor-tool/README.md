Tractor Toolkit

Tractor Toolkit is a planning tool for people who start a tractor work business.

A blueprint tab lists nine business stages with checklists that you can expand, collapse, and print. A rate tab works out an hourly rate and sends it to the quote. A quote tab builds a job quote you can print. A game tab runs a driving game with a restart button.

Live at tractor-tool.vercel.app

Checked every morning by Control Troll, an automated test system that loads every app and clicks through its features.
