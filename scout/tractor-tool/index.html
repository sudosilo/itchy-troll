Sheet Notes // How to read this drawing

About This Blueprint

This sheet maps the complete operating system for a one-operator tractor and land services business: clearing, grading, driveways, and gravel. It is laid out like a civil drawing because that is what it is, a drawing of how the business flows from first phone call to money in the bank.

Yellow-tagged boxes are system stages. Tap one to open its checklists, the documents that stage owns, and word-for-word scripts you can read straight off the phone. Diamonds are decision points where the path splits.

The intent is simple: nothing important lives only in your head. When every stage has its checklist and its script, the business runs the same on your worst day as it does on your best, and it can eventually run with someone else in the seat.

Work the improvement loop at the bottom once a month and bump the revision letter in the title block when the sheet changes.
