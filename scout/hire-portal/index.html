Work eligibility

At this stage an employer only needs two answers: whether you are authorized to work in the US, and whether you need sponsorship.

Be careful if an application demands a passport, birth certificate, green card, or ID scan before any offer. Those documents belong to the I-9 form, which is done after you are hired.

Both answers are required to continue.

Prove you are human

A live face check, also called a liveness check, proves a real person is applying and not a bot or a held up photo. Delivery apps and payment companies use the same kind of check.

Here the video stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the still from the check is only offered as your badge photo.

A real employer tells you who processes your face scan and how long they keep it.

Be careful if a face check also asks for a photo of your ID card before any offer, or if someone sends you a face check link over text chat or a video call.

Hold your device at eye level in good light. Follow the prompts under the circle.
