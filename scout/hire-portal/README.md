Hire Portal

Hire Portal is a practice job application for people who want to learn what a real employer should and should not ask.

The steps are account, resume upload, a check of what was read from it, work eligibility, and signature. A What to share panel on each step explains what is safe to give and warns about requests such as identity documents or fees.
