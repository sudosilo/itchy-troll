Hackercons Roulette

Hackercons Roulette is a clip player for hacker conference talks. It is for anyone who likes to stumble on something new.

Stumble mode plays random 30 second clips from conference talks. You can skip, pause, and keep clips, and kept talks save to a list. Guess mode asks which conference a clip is from and tracks your streak and best. The next clip loads in the background, and dead clips get reported.

Live at hackercons-roulette.vercel.app
