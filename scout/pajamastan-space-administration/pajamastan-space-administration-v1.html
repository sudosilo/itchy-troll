About
Solar wind, Kp, radio flux, storm scales, GOES particle counts, and alerts come from the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center feed. Flares, coronal mass ejections, and storms come from the NASA DONKI event database. The magnetopause shape uses the Shue model driven by live solar wind pressure and Bz. Field lines are a dipole squeezed on the day side and stretched on the night side, which is a teaching picture and not a full physics model. Pole positions are approximate current values.
