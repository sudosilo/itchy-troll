Pajamastan Space Administration

Pajamastan Space Administration is a space weather page for anyone in Pajamastan who wants to watch the sun and the satellites around the earth.

A globe shows live solar activity, the magnetosphere, the poles, and satellite risk. Tabs cover the current state, satellites, the magnetic field, events, and alerts. A solar burst simulator plays a flare, a proton storm, or a coronal mass ejection hitting the magnetosphere at moderate, severe, or Carrington strength. Satellite positions are live and colored by space weather hazard, and a tap shows details and the orbit.

Live at pajamastan-space-administration.vercel.app
