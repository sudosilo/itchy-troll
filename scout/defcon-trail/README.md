DEF CON Trail

DEF CON Trail is a travel survival game in the style of a classic wagon trail game. It is for players who know hacker conference culture.

You manage a crew on the way to Las Vegas. Each day you can continue, charge, rest, talk, hunt for swag, shop, party, gamble, or check supplies. You choose sleep, food, and pace, and your crew health and vibes change as the days go by.

Live at defcon-trail.vercel.app
