Pajamastan

Pajamastan is the official site of a micronation, for its citizens and visitors.

The site has a menu with grouped pages, an atlas, inventory tabs, and a creator seal.

Live at pajamastan.vercel.app

Checked every morning by Control Troll, an automated test system that loads every app and clicks through its features.
