Parkingten

Parkingten is a parking management platform for lot operators and drivers.

Drivers can check in to a spot, and operators have their own page protected by an operator key. The app keeps spots and sessions in a Redis data store. A plate photo is resized before it is analyzed. Smart contract files for parking spots are included in the repo.

Live at parkingten.vercel.app

Checked every morning by Control Troll, an automated test system that loads every app and clicks through its features.
