Chicken Farm

Chicken Farm is a pixel art farm simulator. It is for players who like building and managing a farm.

You pick a starting environment, then place chickens, feed, fences, coops, water sources, pipes, and more. Water flows through pipes and chickens eat, drink, and use coops. Run an egg or meat farm and sell chickens to earn money.

Live at pixelcoop-farm.vercel.app
