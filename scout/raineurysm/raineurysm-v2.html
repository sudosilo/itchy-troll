How it works
The left side of the screen rains in lanes. Every lane drops one kind of raindrop, same color, same size, same speed, on its own beat. The far right side is the storm, everything mixed together.

Lane drops: navy blobs are kick drums, grey specks are hi hats, pale grey is the open hat, orange is the snare, pink is the clap, brown is the tom, lime dust is the shaker, teal is the bass line. The blues, red, green, gold and violet lanes are melody.

Melody cups are tuned by where they sit, low on the left and high on the right, and the arrows in the cup panel shift a cup up or down the scale. Fast drops ring an octave up, slow drops hum, big drops hit harder.

Tap a cup to select it. Drag it to move it. In the cup panel, shrink the width until it only fits under one lane, and tilt it. Cups hold water. A tilted cup pours from its low lip, and a full cup spills over, so you can stack cups and let one drip into another for a second note.

The button in the upper right switches what a tap on empty space does. PLACE drops a new cup. MOVE only selects and drags. ERASE removes a cup you tap.
