raineurysm

raineurysm is a rain music toy. It is for anyone who likes playing with sound on a phone.

You place vessels on the screen and rain falls into them, so each drop plays a note. There are rain lanes, drums, bass, and a storm zone. You can pick the kind of vessel, such as a cup, jug, metal pan, plate, singing bowl, wood block, or ladle. It joins the Stompbox Lab band room, so the rain follows the band beat and shares one scale.
