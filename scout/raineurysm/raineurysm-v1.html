How it works
Rain falls through everything until a vessel catches it. Each vessel is tuned by where it sits, low notes on the left and high notes on the right. Fast drops ring an octave up with a sharp pluck, slow drops hum soft and long, big drops hit louder. Drop color sets the voice: blue is pure, red is warm, green is hollow, gold is bright.

The button in the upper right switches what a tap does. PLACE drops a vessel where you tap. MOVE lets you drag a vessel around. ERASE removes a vessel you tap.
