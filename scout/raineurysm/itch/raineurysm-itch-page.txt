raineurysm

Short description: Catch colored rain in cups and bowls and turn it into music.

Rain falls in every color, and every drop that lands in something plays a note. Set cups, jugs, pans, plates, bowls, wood blocks and ladles under the rain and each one sings in its own voice. The left side rains in steady lanes of kicks, snares, hats, bass and melody on a beat, and the right side is a mixed storm. Fast drops ring higher, big drops hit harder, and a full cup tipped over pours into the next one for a second note.

Simple mode is the rain and your cups. Machine mode lets you build a water machine with faucets that drip on the beat or run a steady humming stream, sprinklers that sweep the room, troughs that carry water sideways, funnels that gather a spray into one stream, and vessels that drip one drop per beat. You can write your own lane patterns, turn the weather into a song with drizzle, downpour and thunder, save scenes, and record what you make.

Puzzle mode has twenty four levels where faucets pour colored water and glass jars want one exact mix. Blue and red make purple, red and gold make orange. Steer the water with troughs and funnels so the right colors meet in each jar, and every drop you route plays its part of the song. Solve levels fast with few parts for a higher run score and post it to the shared high score board under a three letter tag.

Phones can join a band room with other music apps and rain in time with the band.

Plays best in portrait on a phone, and works in a desktop browser too. Tap or click to place, drag to move, tap a piece to change it.
