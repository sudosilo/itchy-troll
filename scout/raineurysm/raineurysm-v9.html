How it works
The left side of the screen rains in lanes. Every lane drops one kind of raindrop, same color, same size, same speed, on its own beat. The far right side is the storm, everything mixed together.

Lane drops: navy blobs are kick drums, grey specks are hi hats, pale grey is the open hat, orange is the snare, pink is the clap, brown is the tom, lime dust is the shaker, teal is the bass line. The blues, red, green, gold and violet lanes are melody. The aqua lane falls in triplets, the pink lane falls in bursts of four, and the orange and mustard lanes side by side are a call and its response.

Vessels: pick what you place in the Vessels section of this menu, or tap a vessel and change it in its panel. The cup plays the drop's own tone, the jug bloops, the pan rings like metal, the plate pings high, the bowl sings long, the wood block knocks, and the ladle tinks. Drum drops play their drum in any vessel.

Melody cups are tuned by where they sit, low on the left and high on the right, and the arrows in the cup panel shift a cup up or down the scale. Fast drops ring an octave up, slow drops hum, big drops hit harder.

Tap a cup to select it. Drag it to move it. In the cup panel, shrink the width until it only fits under one lane, and tilt it. Cups hold water. A tilted cup pours from its low lip, and a full cup spills over, so you can stack cups and let one drip into another for a second note.

The faint blue line across the lanes is the beat line. Lane drops cross it right on the beat, so a cup sitting on that line plays in time. Higher cups catch early, lower cups catch late.

Band room: type the same room code the Stompbox Lab phones use and press Join. The lanes then rain on the band's tempo and bar, so your cups play in time with the room, and every note you catch goes out to the visualizers in the room. Other raineurysm phones in the room share your scale and root note. While you are in a room and the rain is going, this phone counts as a player, so the band loop keeps running.

The button in the upper right switches what a tap on empty space does. PLACE drops a new cup. MOVE only selects and drags. ERASE removes a cup you tap.
