MobbyHill

Climb the wireframe hills and reach the red flags. Drag the wheel to steer, pick D or R, hold Gas. Watch the roll and pitch gauges. Red marks are where the rig goes over.

Tilt the phone to lean the driver. Leaning toward the uphill side keeps you on four wheels. Leaning downhill tips you over. The arrow buttons lean too.
