Delivery Twin Violet

Delivery Twin Violet is a parts inventory app for field crews. It is part of the Delivery Twin family and started as a copy of Field Twin Violet.

It is built around parts. Home has quick actions, and each item has a page with photos, vendors, and reorder levels. An order list tracks what to buy, and items can be moved between locations. It shares one sign in with the other Delivery Twin apps.

Live at delivery-twin-violet.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll, an automated test system that loads every app and clicks through its features.
