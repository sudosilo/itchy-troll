BIRDIXEL

BIRDIXEL is a bird themed territory game with art modes. It is for players and for people who like making pixel art.

In classic mode you claim most of the grid while dodging enemy birds. Free paint lets the bird fly and paint. Stained glass mode draws lead lines with several autopilots, and photo glass turns an uploaded photo into stained glass. You can clear and export your work.

Live at birdix.vercel.app
