Restaurant Design and Setup Survey

Restaurant Design and Setup Survey is a form tool for people planning a new restaurant concept or reviewing an existing one.

It walks you through the concept identity and setup sections, scores an existing concept with a live completeness meter, loads sample concepts, and exports a summary you can copy.

Live at ghost-kitchen-builder.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.
