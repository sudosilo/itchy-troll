Thanks For Watching

Thanks For Watching is a web app for people who upload clips and want them played back in a shuffle. It has profiles and a shared account hub.

It plays a shuffle bucket of uploads and has profiles, badges, and flags. Sign in works with an emailed code typed into the app. A trim editor removes dead air automatically, lets you split and cut by hand, and exports in the browser.

Live at thanks-for-watching.vercel.app.
