How this works
Every video is one minute or less. Videos go into a shared bucket and get dealt out to viewers starting with the ones that have been seen the least, so nothing snowballs. The player never shows who made a video. No ads, no watermarks, no counts. Badges go to viewers for watching widely, and they only show up on profiles for anyone who goes looking. If a video breaks the rules, flag it, and enough flags pull it out of the bucket.
