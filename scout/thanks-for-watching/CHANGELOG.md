Thanks For Watching changelog

2026-11-12, v2026.11.12. Released Thanks For Watching version one with a shuffle bucket player, uploads, profiles, badges, and flags. Version two added a shared accounts hub and email sign in. Added a log in button at the top of the menu and a way to reclaim a handle left on an anonymous account. Version three added a trim editor with automatic dead air removal, manual split and cut, and in browser export. Version four signs in with an emailed code typed into the app instead of a link.
