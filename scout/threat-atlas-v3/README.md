Threat Atlas

Threat Atlas is an interactive globe of security threats. It is for researchers who want a visual map of incidents and places.

The globe rotates by drag, and tapping a country opens intel. Layers toggle threats, conferences, defense and cyber, US government cyber, satellites, and US military bases. Results filter by threat class and year. Intel and US states views list incidents with threat levels.

Live at threat-atlas-two.vercel.app
