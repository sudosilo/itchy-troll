LV Liquor Intel

LV Liquor Intel is a map of liquor stores, casino bars, and other bars around Las Vegas. It is for anyone scouting where drinks are sold.

Red markers are standalone liquor, wine, and beer stores, and a blue ring marks a chain. Orange markers are casino and resort liquor, and blue markers are bars, pubs, and nightclubs. You can switch layers, filter by ownership and by region, search, and see a list sorted by distance from the map center. Tap a marker for details, fit the visible markers, center on yourself, reload the data, or export the standalone stores as a CSV file. Data comes from OpenStreetMap and is cached on the device for twelve hours.

Live at lv-liquor-map.vercel.app
