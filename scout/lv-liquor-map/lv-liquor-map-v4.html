About
Store data comes from Overture Maps open places data, built from Meta, Microsoft, Foursquare and business registry listings, baked into this app. Live OpenStreetMap data is merged in on top whenever its servers answer.

Red retail liquor, wine and beer stores.
Blue ring marks a chain or branded store.
Orange casino and resort liquor, including any shop or bar inside a mapped casino footprint.
Blue bars, pubs, lounges and nightclubs.
Purple grocery, convenience and drug stores, which sell package liquor in Nevada. Off by default.
