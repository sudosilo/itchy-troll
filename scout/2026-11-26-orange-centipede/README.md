Centipede Text Edition

Centipede Text Edition is a browser arcade game for people who like classic shooters.

A centipede arcade shooter drawn entirely in letters. You play on keyboard or phone and post your score. It has a Career mode with 10 levels and a Puzzle mode with 12 puzzles that were checked by a solver.
