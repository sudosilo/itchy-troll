Coffee Autosim

Coffee Autosim is a coffee shop simulator. It is for people who want to watch how a shop floor flows.

A shop floor is built with stations and stick figure baristas. Tickets are placed in slots and baristas walk to stations to fill them on their own. House brew is used and refilled. Speed buttons run the sim at half, one, two, and four times.

Live at coffee-autosim.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.
