2026-10-30, v2026.10.30. Added a rearrangeable floor and a visual pass.

Rearranging. A REARRANGE button in the top bar turns on placement mode. Every moveable item on the floor picks up a dashed orange outline: the wall stations (food warmer, hot water, espresso, steam wand, cold brew, ice, blender, recipe printer), the service counters, all eleven milk and syrup chips, and the seven fixed fixtures (both KDS screens, the Zebra label printer, the recipe printer, the drive-thru window, the lobby seating, and the house brew gauge). Drag any of them anywhere on the grid and drop it. Positions are stored in localStorage under coffee-autosim:layout:v1, so a layout survives a reload. SNAP ON keeps drops on the half-grid cell; turn it off for free placement. RESET LAYOUT clears the saved layout and reloads into the original corrected rail layout. Keyboard: R toggles placement mode, Escape leaves it.

Reaching matters. The barista pathing reads station coordinates live, so moving a station also moves where staff walk to and where tickets pop up. Dropping a station sends any barista standing on it back to the counter instead of leaving them stuck inside.

Touch. Dragging uses pointer events, so mouse, finger, and pen all work, with setPointerCapture so a fast flick doesn't lose the item. The barista slider now has a 22px thumb with a pressed state.

Visual pass. Type in the sim was set between 3.5 and 7px, which was too small to read on a normal screen. Type and the containers holding it were scaled up together so nothing overflows. The KDS screens grew from 140 and 120 pixels to 176 and 150 with larger ticket names, tags, and step labels. Contrast fixes where text was effectively invisible: completed ticket steps, the active-order counters on both KDS headers, the label-printer caption, printer status text, and the KDS section labels. The floor plan labels, queue bar, printer boxes, road, and leaderboard all went up a size. Controls: a bolder title, larger speed buttons with hover and focus states, a gradient-filled slider track, and a larger barista count. Added a reduced-motion rule that stops the decorative animations while leaving informational colour changes, and a 720px breakpoint that stacks the stats into three across, stacks the printer strip, and turns both KDS screens into single-column scrolling lists.
