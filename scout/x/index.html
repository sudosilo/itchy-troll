Mary Jane-Man

You are a weed leaf. Collect all the nugs.
Avoid the pipes or they will smoke you.
Grab a Golden Nug to scare them.

How To Play

You are a cannabis leaf loose in the maze. Eat every green nug to clear the level. Four lit pipes named Bubba, Mary, Cheech and Chong roam the board, and they alternate between chasing you directly and scattering at random, so the pressure comes in waves rather than constantly.

Touching a pipe costs you a life and you start with three. Eating one of the four Golden Nugs in the corners flips things for a while: the pipes turn blue and run from you, and you can eat them for 200 points each. They blink white just before the effect wears off, so that is your cue to back away. A regular nug is worth 10 points and a Golden Nug is worth 50 on its own.

The row through the middle of the maze wraps around, so leaving the left edge brings you out on the right. Use it to break a chase. Clear the board and the next level starts with faster pipes.

Controls

Use the D-pad below the board, or swipe across the board itself, or use the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard. Turns are queued, so you can press a direction slightly before the opening and the leaf takes it as soon as the turn is legal.

About

Mary Jane-Man was designed and built by Green. The whole thing was written and shipped from the Green phone, developing in Termux on Android and deploying straight to Vercel from the command line, with no desktop involved at any point.

The original source for this game lives in Google Drive. It is no longer held in Claude, so Drive is the authoritative copy and anything you need to fork, audit or continue from should be pulled from there rather than from a chat history.
