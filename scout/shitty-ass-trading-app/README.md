Shitty Ass Trading App

This app tracks stock prices for restaurant chains and the companies that supply them. It is for someone who follows the food industry.

Setup takes one or more free API keys, or you can skip it and use a shared key. It tracks a ticker list with week, month, and year views and a one day chart with a small preview on each tile. You can sort by manual order, color, or biggest movers, and filter by recent news. News, recalls, trade magazine stories, SEC filings, tariff notices, commodity prices, insider filings, and congressional trading search all hang off each ticker. A sentiment score pulls the signals together. Read aloud speaks the info text, and sync codes move settings between devices.
