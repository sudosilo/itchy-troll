Field Dispatch

Field Dispatch is a field service dispatch game for anyone who likes running a crew on a real map.

You take tickets from an open board and send crews along real road routes. Cameras need repairs, satellite passes come over, and plate reader service contracts can be taken or declined. Tabs show tickets, crew, stock, cameras, and the shop, and the game can be restarted at any time.
