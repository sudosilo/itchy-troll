Scope Listener

Scope Listener is an oscilloscope for anyone who wants to line up music with what a phone microphone hears.

It compares a local file or YouTube video against the phone mic and shows the offset and drift. Views switch between overlay, split, X-Y, difference, spectrum, waterfall, radial, and correlation. A listen offset mode, half second nudge, auto track, loop, and trim buttons help line things up. An autopilot mode now identifies the song, loads the reference, locates the spot, and tracks and logs the sample level without any taps.

Live at scope-listener.vercel.app
