Bee Wars

Bee Wars is a small strategy game for players who like watching a colony grow. It runs in a browser.

You lead a bee colony against a rival. Pollen your bees bring home hatches new bees. Workers gather pollen, soldiers fight, and scouts fly fast and see far. Land you hold is tinted your color and makes your soldiers hit harder. You can pause, step, zoom, change settings, and start a new map.

Live at beewars.vercel.app

Latest change. Created Bee Wars, a territory control game with two bee colonies in one meadow. You win by growing to 21 bees or by killing the rival queen. Send workers, soldiers, and scouts around the map, paint walls, and pick what each new bee will be.
