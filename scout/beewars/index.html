BEE WARS

Two colonies, one meadow. Win by growing to 21 bees or by killing the rival queen.

Every 50 pollen your bees bring home hatches a new bee. Hive pollen never goes down. HATCH picks what comes out next: AUTO, WORKER, SOLDIER or SCOUT.

WORKERS gather pollen and flee from enemy soldiers. SOLDIERS have 3 health and fight. SCOUTS fly twice as fast and see farther.

Pick WORKERS, SOLDIERS or SCOUTS, then tap the map to send that caste there. Tap your own hive with SOLDIERS to guard the queen. Tap the rival hive with SOLDIERS to attack it.

Land you hold is tinted your color. In your land your soldiers hit twice as hard, flowers regrow faster, and enemy workers will not forage.

WALL paints tiles your bees refuse to cross. Your queen stings anything that gets close. Spiders hide in the grass.
